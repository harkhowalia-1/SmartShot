SmartShot

The camera that
waits for everyone.

It takes the photo the moment everyone is facing the camera, holding still and not blinking. No self-timer race. You're in the photo too.

Download View on GitHub

Press once.
Get the shot.

Set your phone down, tap the shutter and join the group. SmartShot handles the rest.

👥

Everyone, ready.

Faces, gaze, blinks and movement are checked for every person in the frame, many times a second.

🔁

Keeps shooting.

A new photo every time the whole group is ready again, until you stop it.

🙋

Waits for you.

With the back camera, it holds the first photo until you've stepped into the shot.

💻

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The same smart camera everywhere, including your Mac's built-in camera or your iPhone as a webcam.

Hold still · 2 taken

Shoots at exactly the right moment.

When everyone is ready, the brackets turn yellow and the shutter ring fills as the group holds still. The photo is taken on the first frame where nobody is mid-blink.

2 of 3 ready

Knows who's not ready yet.

Live hints show who's looking away, moving or blinking, and the banner counts how many people are ready. No more "wait, I wasn't looking."

Pro formats. Built in.

HEIF or ProRAW at 12, 24 or 48 megapixels, as your device allows, with the same processing as the built-in camera. 48 MP uses the main camera and switches lenses for .5× and telephoto automatically.

🔍

Zoom and focus.

Lens buttons, pinch to zoom, and tap anywhere to focus and set exposure.

🖼️

A viewer you already know.

Swipe, pinch and double-tap through your photos, check the details, and share the original file.

✂️

Edit without regret.

Exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth, crop, straighten, rotate and flip. Every edit is non-destructive and can be reverted at any time.

🔒

Private by design.

Everything SmartShot sees is analysed on your device. Camera frames are never stored or sent anywhere. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Your photos go to your own library, and nowhere else.
