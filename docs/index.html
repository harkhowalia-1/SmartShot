How it works.

1

Set it down.

Prop up your iPhone, iPad or Mac and press the shutter once. No countdown to set.

2

Get in the shot.

Take your time. It waits until everyone is in place, settled and looking, then takes the photo.

3

Keep posing.

Change it up. A new photo every time the group settles again, so you can pick the best one later.

100%

Private.

Everything happens on your device. No account, no cloud, and nothing ever leaves it.
