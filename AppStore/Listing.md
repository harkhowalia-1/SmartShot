## App Review information

Sign-in required: **No**. Contact: your name, email and phone.

**Notes**
SmartShot is a camera app that takes a group photo automatically when everyone in the frame is facing the camera, holding still and not blinking.

To test: open the app, allow Camera and Photos access, and press the shutter (it turns into a stop button). Point the camera at one or more people, or at a photo of a group on another screen. Yellow brackets appear on faces that are ready; the photo is taken when everyone is ready, and again each time they become ready, until you press stop. Taken photos appear in the thumbnail (bottom left), which opens the built-in viewer and editor.

All face analysis uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. The app has no account, no network access and collects no data.
