**Description**
Set it down. Join the shot.

No timer to set and no sprint back to the group. Prop up your iPhone, iPad or Mac, press the shutter once and walk into the picture. SmartShot takes the photo when everyone is in place, settled and looking, and keeps shooting while you pose.

NO TIMER NEEDED
• Press once, then take your time getting into the shot
• With the back camera, it waits for you to step in

IT KNOWS WHEN YOU'RE READY
• Watches every face: in position, holding still, looking at the camera
• Live hints show who's still looking away, moving or blinking
• Takes the photo on a frame where nobody is mid-blink

KEEP POSING
• A new photo every time the group settles again, until you press stop
• Change poses as much as you like, then pick your favourite

FULL QUALITY
• HEIF or ProRAW at up to 48 MP, as your device allows
• Zoom buttons, pinch to zoom, tap to focus and exposure, flash
• Front and back cameras; on Mac, any built-in or external camera

A PHOTOS VIEWER YOU ALREADY KNOW
• Swipe, pinch and double-tap through your shots
• Info, favourites, share the original, hide and delete
• Non-destructive editor: exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth, crop, straighten, rotate and flip

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Faces are analysed on your device and never leave it. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

## App Review information

Sign-in required: **No**. Contact: your name, email and phone.

**Notes**
SmartShot is a camera app that takes a group photo automatically when everyone in the frame is facing the camera, holding still and not blinking.

To test: open the app, allow Camera and Photos access, and press the shutter (it turns into a stop button). Point the camera at one or more people, or at a photo of a group on another screen. Yellow brackets appear on faces that are ready; the photo is taken when everyone is ready, and again each time they become ready, until you press stop. Taken photos appear in the thumbnail (bottom left), which opens the built-in viewer and editor.

All face analysis uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. The app has no account, no network access and collects no data.
